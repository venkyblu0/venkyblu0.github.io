Privacy policy: youtube-connect

Last updated: 12 October 2026

youtube-connect is a personal tool used only by its owner on their own computer, for their own YouTube channel.

  • What it accesses: through Google's YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API, the owner's own channel, videos, analytics reports and comments.
  • Where data is kept: only on the owner's computer. The sign-in file stays on that computer and is never shared.
  • Sharing: no data is sold, shared, or sent to anyone other than Google's own APIs.
  • Revoking access: access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

youtube-connect's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
